Privacy Policy — Simple Sound Profiles

Last updated: 21 September 2026

The short version

Simple Sound Profiles does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app has no internet permission. It is technically incapable of sending anything anywhere, including to the developer.

What the app stores

Your profiles — their names, icons, ringer modes and volume levels — are saved in a database on your own device. They never leave it unless you choose to export them.

If you use Export profiles, the app writes a file to a location you pick using Android's own file picker. That file goes only where you send it. The app does not upload it, read it back later, or know what you do with it.

Permissions, and why

  • Do Not Disturb access — required by Android before any app may switch the ringer to

    • Vibrate or Silent, or set ring volume to zero. Used only when you apply a profile. It is not used to read your notifications, and the app cannot see their contents.

  • Vibrate — the short buzz confirming a profile was applied. You can turn this off.
  • File access — none requested. Export and import use Android's Storage Access

    • Framework, which grants the app access to the single file you choose and nothing else.

    What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising
  • No accounts, sign-in or cloud sync
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No tracking of any kind
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, call logs or messages

Android backup

If you have Android's backup enabled, your profile database may be included in your device backup or phone-to-phone transfer, in the same way as other app data. That is handled entirely by Android and your Google account under Google's terms, not by this app. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Privacy Policy — Hive Purge

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The short version

Hive Purge has no accounts and never asks for your email or any other contact details. Your progress stays on your own device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data as described below, and sells one optional purchase, Remove Ads, through Google Play. Nothing is sold.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress (your heroes, their levels and talents, your gear, the Reactor, your gold, the planets you've cleared, daily rewards and missions) and your settings are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to the developer.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data. It is not included in Android's device backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free to play to the end, and shows two kinds of ads:

  • An ad between runs: a full-screen ad after a run, none until you've cleared your first planet, none in your first two runs each time you open the game, and at most one every 3 minutes. Watching three rewarded ads within an hour earns 3 hours without them.
  • Optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch one to revive once in a run, to double a run's rewards, to deal new upgrade cards on a level-up, to open a free crate, to double a daily mission's reward, or to reset a hero's talents.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location (country or city)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads
  • App performance and diagnostic information, such as launch time and stability

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads load. Where a privacy law gives you a way to change your choice, the game's Settings has Privacy options. You can reset your advertising ID or delete it in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Remove Ads (Google Play)

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that ends the ads between runs for good; the rewarded ads stay, for their rewards. Google Play handles the payment; the game never sees your payment details. The game asks Google Play only whether Remove Ads was bought on your Google account, each time it starts, so that a new phone or a reinstall gets it back. Google Play keeps a record of each order (such as its number, date, price and country), which the developer can see in the Google Play Console. See Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

The game can remind you when a free crate is ready. Your phone schedules and shows these reminders itself: nothing is sent anywhere. Turn them off in the game's Settings (Notifications) or in Android's settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, chat or cloud saves
  • No analytics of its own: nothing about how you play is sent to the developer
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, messages or precise location
  • No sale of personal data

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to check Remove Ads with Google Play
  • Google Play Billing: to buy and restore Remove Ads
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above
  • Notifications, and running at startup: to remind you when a free crate is ready, even after your phone restarts (can be turned off in Settings → Notifications)
  • Vibration: for small vibrations at moments in the game (can be turned off in Settings → Vibration)

Data retention and deletion

Data collected by AdMob is kept by Google under its own retention settings. Because the game has no accounts, the developer cannot look up or identify your data. To stop all collection, uninstall the app; you can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

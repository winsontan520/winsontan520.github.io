Privacy Policy — Reborn Adventure: Idle RPG

Last updated: 12 October 2026

The short version

Reborn Adventure has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Your game is saved on your own device. To show optional rewarded ads, count how the game is played and fix crashes, it uses Google services (AdMob and Firebase), which collect some device and usage data as described below. We don't sell your data, and you can opt out of personalised ads.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: your hero, companions, gear, stages cleared and settings are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to the developer.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data. If Android backup is on, it may be included in your device backup, handled by Android and your Google account, not by this app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one for a bonus, such as doubling an offline reward. There are no banner ads and no ads you have to watch. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location (country or city)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads load. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your privacy choices

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: you can change your consent at any time. Open the game's Settings and tap Privacy choices.
  • US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the use of your data for personalised ads, which some of these laws call "selling" or "sharing" personal information. Open the game's Settings and tap Privacy choices.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how it is played and to fix problems. They may collect:

  • Game events, such as stages cleared or lost, companions recruited and ads watched, with the stage or item involved
  • An app instance ID created for this install, and device information (model, operating system, language, country)
  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, with technical details of the device

This data is not linked to your name or contact details, because the game never has them. Analytics follows the same consent answer as ads in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. See Firebase privacy and security.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, chat or cloud saves
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, messages or precise location
  • No sale of personal data for money; ads may be personalised unless you opt out (see Your privacy choices)
  • No in-app purchases at present

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and send analytics and crash reports
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above

Data retention and deletion

Data collected by AdMob and Firebase is kept by Google under its own retention settings (for Firebase Analytics, 14 months; for Crashlytics, 90 days). Because the game has no accounts, the developer cannot look up or identify your data. To stop all collection, uninstall the app; you can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

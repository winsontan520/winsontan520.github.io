Privacy Policy — Batik Bloom

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The short version

Batik Bloom: Wax & Dye Puzzle has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Your progress is saved on your own device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data as described below. Nothing is sold.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: levels unlocked, stars earned, the cloths in your collection, and your settings are stored in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not uploaded to the developer.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data. If Android backup is on, it may be included in your device backup, handled by Android and your Google account, not by this app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads:

  • Optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one for an extra hint, after the free hints for that level are used. Undo is always free.
  • Interstitial ads: a full-screen ad shown occasionally between levels, from level 10 onwards.

There are no banner ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location (country or city)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads
  • App performance and diagnostic information, such as launch time and stability

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads load, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, chat or cloud saves
  • No gameplay statistics or analytics are sent to the developer
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, messages or precise location
  • No sale of personal data
  • No in-app purchases at present

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads
  • Vibration: for small vibrations when you press wax, dip the cloth and complete a level (can be turned off in Settings → Vibration)
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above

Data retention and deletion

Data collected by AdMob is kept by Google under its own retention settings. Because the game has no accounts, the developer cannot look up or identify your data. To stop all collection, uninstall the app; you can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
